Data Records

This section describes the file types, organizational structure, and storage format of the dataset. The public release package contains four components: raw wind power observations at 15 min resolution, processed wind power products at 15 min and 1 h resolution, site-matched ERA5 reanalysis data, and station metadata tables. Raw observations are stored in the `raw_wind_power` folder, while the cleaned 15 min product and the derived 1 h product are stored in the `processed_wind_power` folder. The 1 h product is generated by retaining only the top-of-hour records from the cleaned 15 min series, rather than by hourly averaging. The `era5_data` folder contains the matched ERA5 files for all sites and the representative-site subset with expanded meteorological variables, together with a public manifest file. The metadata folder includes `public_station_index.csv`, which provides the public station label, city name, county-level geographical location, installed capacity, and terrain type for each wind farm. To align public data disclosure with the manuscript naming convention, all public filenames, power-data headers, and figure labels follow the same county-level station naming system, and precise longitude and latitude are not released in the public package.
